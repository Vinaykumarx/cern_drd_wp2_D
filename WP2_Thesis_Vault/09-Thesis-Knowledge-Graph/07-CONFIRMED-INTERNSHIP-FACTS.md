## Official DRD8 WP2 context

DRD8 Work Package 2 is officially titled **Low-mass Mechanics and Thermal
Management**. Its scope includes advanced materials, material qualification,
data collation, inter-laboratory certification, and development of a database
through which material data can be accessed.

Official WP2 page: <https://drd8.web.cern.ch/work-package-2/>

Rajendra Karmarkar (Purdue University)**. Their leadership does not by itself
