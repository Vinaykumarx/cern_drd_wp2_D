## Central thesis argument

The internship evolved from a lightweight Mac prototype for multimodal scientific
PDF retrieval into a modular, production-oriented knowledge-structuring platform.
The engineering contribution was the progressive adaptation of extraction,
chunking, storage, retrieval, user interface, and validation to increasingly
demanding scientific and infrastructure requirements.

## Three-act narrative

1. **Constrained discovery:** build and debug a CPU-first prototype on a Mac.
2. **Production restructuring:** migrate storage, separate services, support multiple documents, and improve grounding.
3. **Compute-enabled scaling:** extend extraction and visual understanding, audit production risks, and begin the Docling migration.

## Important interpretation rule

The archive contains implemented features, proposals, future roadmaps, and
presentation claims. The thesis must label these separately. A planned capability
must not be presented as a completed benchmark.
