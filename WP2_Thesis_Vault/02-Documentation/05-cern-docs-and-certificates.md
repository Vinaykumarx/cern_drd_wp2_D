Exactly — CERN has multiple internal platforms depending on the type of document or certificate:  

1. **EDH (Electronic Document Handling)** – [https://edh.cern.ch](https://edh.cern.ch)  
   - Mainly for official administrative documents, approvals, and HR forms.  
   - You can search by your name or CERN ID to find documents like contracts, letters, or official certificates.  

2. **Training Portal / HR-Training** – [https://training.cern.ch](https://training.cern.ch)  
   - For course completions, e-learning, safety training certificates, and other training-related documents.  

3. **CERN CDS / CDS Document Server** – [https://cds.cern.ch](https://cds.cern.ch)  
   - Mostly research papers, internal reports, theses, and conference documents. Not always personal certificates.  

4. **HR Portal / HR Services** – [https://hr-services.web.cern.ch](https://hr-services.web.cern.ch)  
   - For employment-related certificates (internship attestations, work references, etc.).  

**Tip:**  
- EDH is usually the go-to for anything official signed by CERN.  
- Training portal is for anything course-related.  
- You can log in with your CERN SSO to all of these.

To access your documents and certificates on the CERN platform, you generally need to use **CERN’s HR and training portals**. Here’s the step-by-step:

1. **Log in to CERN accounts**:  
   Go to [CERN Single Sign-On (SSO)](https://sso.cern.ch) and log in with your CERN credentials.

2. **Check HR records (for official documents)**:  
   - Go to [HR Services Portal](https://hr-services.web.cern.ch/).  
   - Look for sections like **“My Records”**, **“Documents”**, or **“Certificates”**.  
   - You should find employment, internship, or training certificates here.

3. **Check training portal (for completed courses/certifications)**:  
   - Go to [CERN Training Portal (HR-Training)](https://training.cern.ch/).  
   - Log in and check **“My Training Records”** or **“My Certificates”**.  
   - You can download completion certificates for courses you finished.

4. **Alternative document access**:  
   - If you had CERN e-learning or safety courses, the certificate PDFs are usually available for download after you pass the course.  
   - Sometimes certificates are sent to your CERN email, so check your inbox as well.

5. **If you can’t find it**:  
   - Contact **HR support**: `HR-Services.Helpdesk@cern.ch`.  
   - Provide your CERN ID and the specific certificate or document you need—they can retrieve it for you.
