**CERN campus — straight facts**

CERN (the **European Organization for Nuclear Research**) is one of the world’s biggest and most important physics research labs. Its main campus is just outside **Geneva, Switzerland**, right on the France–Switzerland border in the municipality of **Meyrin**. citeturn0search32turn0search37

### What the CERN “campus” actually is
- It’s not a single building you walk around like a museum — it’s a sprawling **research complex with offices, labs, workshops, cafeterias, and facilities** spread across several interconnected sites. citeturn0search3  
- CERN spans **Meyrin** (the administrative and scientific hub) and **Prévessin** (technical facilities), and beneath much of it lies the **27 km tunnel of the Large Hadron Collider (LHC)** — the world’s biggest particle accelerator. citeturn0search3turn0search47  
- There’s a mix of surface buildings and massive underground infrastructure dedicated to physics experiments like **ATLAS, CMS, ALICE, and LHCb** along the accelerator ring. citeturn0search3

### Visiting CERN
- CERN does public outreach: the **Science Gateway** visitor centre offers exhibitions and activities explaining particle physics and CERN’s work to the general public. Entry is **free but requires registration**. citeturn0search7turn0search20  
- Guided tours of parts of the lab are also free but have high demand — they usually need advance booking or on-site registration. citeturn0search1turn0search16  
- You *cannot* wander around the active campus on your own — access to working labs and underground sites is restricted and typically only allowed as part of an organised tour. citeturn0search17

### Practical Info (Geneva area)
- Address for the visitor reception is **1, Esplanade des Particules, 1217 Meyrin, Switzerland**. citeturn0search0  
- From **Geneva** centre: tram **line 18** goes directly to CERN and is the easiest public transport option. citeturn0search9turn0search13  
- The Science Gateway exhibits are usually open **Tuesday to Sunday** (closed Mondays). citeturn0search20
